# Curated Opportunities

This page collects positions relevant to MEMS, thin films, materials characterization, biomedical engineering, and medical technology.

## How to judge job fit

Prioritize job advertisements containing several of these terms:

- AlN, piezoelectric thin films, MEMS, microsystems, acoustic sensors, ultrasonic devices.
- ALD, sputtering, PVD, thin-film deposition, wafer processing.
- FTIR, XRD, SEM, AFM, ellipsometry, wafer bow, stress measurement.
- Materials characterization, failure analysis, morphology, crystallography, surface analysis.
- MATLAB, Python, data analysis, optical modelling, signal processing.
- Medical devices, verification, validation, calibration, quality assurance, ISO-based workflows.

## Priority categories

| Priority | Vacancy characteristics | Profile fit |
|---|---|---|
| Very high | AlN/piezoelectric films, MEMS, FTIR/XRD/SEM, acoustic sensors, thin-film R&D | Direct thesis and instrument match |
| High | Materials analysis, medical-device R&D, verification/validation, laboratory engineering | Strong research and clinical-engineering match |
| Medium | Biomedical imaging, MATLAB/Python, medical technology, clinical equipment | Relevant previous project and work experience |
| Selective | General quality or production roles without R&D, materials, or medical-device relevance | Apply only when development potential is clear |

## Application strategy

Use the full CV for research and engineering positions. For each application, tailor the first third of the CV and the cover letter to the advertisement's top technical keywords.

For a thin-film/MEMS role, lead with AlN, FTIR, XRD, SEM, wafer bow, ALD, sputtering, and MATLAB.

For a medical-device role, lead with equipment validation, calibration, medical systems, QA documentation, preventive maintenance, and clinical engineering.

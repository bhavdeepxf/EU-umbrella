# Target Organisations

This is a structured list of organisations worth monitoring for roles in MEMS, thin films, materials analysis, sensors, biomedical engineering, and medical technology.

## Research institutes

| Organisation type | Why it may fit |
|---|---|
| Applied research institutes | Strong fit for MEMS, microsystems, sensor engineering, materials, and industry-facing R&D |
| University research groups | Good source of funded PhD and scientific employee positions |
| Medical-technology research centres | Relevant for translational devices, biomedical systems, imaging, and clinical applications |
| Materials and surface-science groups | Relevant for AlN, thin films, spectroscopy, XRD, SEM, and deposition processes |

## Industry segments

| Segment | Typical opportunities |
|---|---|
| Sensors and microsystems | MEMS development, sensor design, acoustic/ultrasonic devices, process development |
| Semiconductor and thin-film manufacturing | ALD, sputtering, film characterization, wafer processes, metrology |
| Medical devices | Biomedical R&D, verification, validation, quality, regulatory support, systems engineering |
| Analytical instruments | Application engineering, customer support, measurement development, laboratory engineering |
| Dental and implant technology | Biomaterials, osseointegration, surface engineering, quality, product development |

## Suggested monitoring routine

- Check official career pages weekly.
- Create alerts for targeted keywords.
- Save each promising vacancy in a private application tracker.
- Identify the research group leader or hiring manager only after confirming that a vacancy is relevant.
- Tailor each application to the research methods or device technologies specified in the advertisement.

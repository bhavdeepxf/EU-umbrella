## Professional experience

### MSc Master Thesis – Acoustic MEMS Research

**Hahn-Schickard-Gesellschaft, Villingen-Schwenningen, Germany**  
**04/2025–10/2025 | Grade: 1.0**

- Developed reflection-mode FTIR protocols for AlN-coated 3D MEMS membranes based on Si/SiO₂/Poly-Si structures with trench widths of 10–80 µm.
- Extracted phonon modes to investigate residual stress and crystal orientation on vertical sidewalls.
- Established a 90° sidewall-mounting and aperture-optimization workflow on a Bruker Hyperion 2000 microscope to improve measurement reproducibility.
- Correlated FTIR phonon-peak shifts with XRD θ–2θ scans and rocking curves using a Bruker D8 Advance.
- Evaluated c-lattice parameters, crystallite size, and texture ratio for c-axis-oriented AlN films.
- Compared ALD and RF magnetron-sputtered AlN films, focusing on the trade-off between ALD sidewall conformality and sputtered (002) texture/non-uniform coverage.
- Used SEM cross-sections on a Zeiss Crossbeam 550, wafer-bow measurements on an E&H MX203, and Fresnel-based MATLAB reflectance simulations to support the analysis.

## Research projects

### Detection of out-of-body images in laparoscopic videos

Developed MATLAB pipelines to detect out-of-body frames in laparoscopic videos using supervised classifiers. Extracted colour-histogram, motion, and specular-highlight features and evaluated SVM, decision-tree, and neural-network models on labelled surgical datasets.

### Automated detection of maculopathy in SD-OCT images

**Bachelor thesis**

Developed a MATLAB pipeline for automated screening of diabetic retinopathy, diabetic macular oedema, and age-related macular degeneration in SD-OCT images.

Segmented retinal layers, extracted statistical and texture features, and trained a multilayer SVM classifier that achieved 93% accuracy, 80% sensitivity, and 100% specificity on the labelled dataset.

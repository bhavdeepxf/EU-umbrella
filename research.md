# Research and Technical Projects

## AlN thin films on 3D MEMS membranes

**Research area:** Acoustic microsystems, thin films, and non-planar surface characterization.

### Research question

How can spectroscopic and structural characterization methods be combined to understand AlN films deposited on vertical and non-planar MEMS surfaces?

### Approach

- Designed a reflection-mode FTIR workflow for 3D AlN-coated membranes.
- Developed a repeatable 90° sidewall mounting arrangement.
- Optimized aperture positioning on a Bruker Hyperion 2000 microscope.
- Measured phonon features associated with AlN films on vertical sidewalls.
- Compared FTIR peak shifts with XRD θ–2θ scans and rocking curves.
- Used SEM cross-sections and wafer-bow data to compare deposition outcomes.
- Applied Fresnel-based MATLAB simulations to support interpretation of reflectance behaviour.

### Technical contribution

The project created a multi-method characterization workflow linking optical response, phonon behaviour, crystallographic texture, morphology, and film stress-related measurements.

The work achieved a final master’s thesis grade of **1.0**.

## ALD versus RF magnetron sputtering

This comparison focused on the practical trade-off between the conformality of ALD coatings on sidewalls and the strong (002) texture and coverage uniformity observed for sputtered AlN films.

Relevant evaluation factors included:

- Sidewall coverage and conformality.
- Crystallographic orientation.
- Surface and cross-sectional morphology.
- Film-stress-related wafer bow.
- Optical response and reflectance simulation.
- Suitability for future acoustic-MEMS device development.

## Automated analysis of laparoscopic videos

Developed MATLAB-based image-processing and machine-learning pipelines to identify out-of-body frames in laparoscopic video.

The objective was to support tool-in-body frame detection and reduce avoidable distraction during surgical-video review.

Methods included colour-histogram features, motion cues, specular-highlight analysis, and supervised classifiers such as SVM, decision trees, and neural-network models.

## Automated maculopathy detection in SD-OCT images

Developed a MATLAB pipeline for screening retinal images associated with diabetic retinopathy, diabetic macular oedema, and age-related macular degeneration.

The project included retinal-layer segmentation, statistical and texture-feature extraction, and multilayer SVM classification.

Reported performance on the labelled dataset was:

- Accuracy: 93%.
- Sensitivity: 80%.
- Specificity: 100%.

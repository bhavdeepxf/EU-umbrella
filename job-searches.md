# Job Searches

## Priority 1 — PhD and research

### EURAXESS

EURAXESS is the main European source for research jobs, doctoral roles, and funding opportunities. Use its filters for country, field, researcher profile, sector, position type, and funding programme.

- [EURAXESS job search](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/search)
- [EURAXESS Germany](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/search?country=Germany)

Recommended searches to run manually:

```text
MEMS
acoustic sensor
ultrasonic
Aluminum Nitride
AlN
piezoelectric thin films
thin film characterization
FTIR
XRD
SEM
surface characterization
biomedical microsystems
medical devices
```

Recommended filters:

```text
Country: Germany first, then selected European countries
Researcher Profile: First Stage Researcher (R1)
Fields: Engineering and Technology; Physical Sciences; Medical and Health Sciences
Sector: Higher Education Institution; Public Research Institution; Research Laboratory; Large Enterprise
```

### Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

- [MSCA jobs](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/jobs)

Use this for funded PhD and doctoral opportunities. The vacancies are published through EURAXESS.

## Priority 2 — Industry and engineering roles

### EURES

- [EURES job search](https://europa.eu/eures/portal/jv-se/home?lang=en&pageCode=find_a_job)

Search separately using:

```text
MEMS engineer
microsystems engineer
sensor development engineer
thin film engineer
materials engineer
surface characterization engineer
medical device engineer
verification validation engineer
biomedical engineer
application engineer spectroscopy
```

Use country filters rather than attempting to collect or scrape vacancy data. The official EURES vacancy database is updated daily.

## Weekly routine

1. Run Priority 1 searches on EURAXESS and MSCA.
2. Run Priority 2 searches on EURES and targeted employer career pages.
3. Add promising roles to `shortlist.md`.
4. Score each role using `job-scorecard.md`.
5. Move roles scoring 70 or more to `application-command-center`.
6. Archive expired roles once the deadline passes.

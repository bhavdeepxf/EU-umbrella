# Technical Capabilities

## Materials and thin films

- Aluminum Nitride (AlN) thin-film characterization.
- Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD).
- RF magnetron sputtering.
- Si/SiO₂/Poly-Si MEMS structures.
- Sidewall and non-planar surface analysis.
- Crystallographic texture and morphology assessment.
- Film-stress-related wafer-bow analysis.

## Measurement and instrumentation

- Bruker Hyperion 2000 FTIR microscope.
- Bruker D8 Advance XRD.
- Zeiss Crossbeam 550 SEM.
- E&H MX203 wafer-bow measurement system.
- INSTRON tensile/compression testing systems.
- Diagnostic imaging, cardiology, and critical-care equipment.

## Engineering and quality

- Equipment installation and commissioning.
- Calibration and validation.
- Preventive maintenance.
- Failure analysis.
- Laboratory documentation.
- Audit preparation and non-conformity reduction.

## Software and analytics

- Image and video processing.
- SVM, decision trees, and neural-network models.
- Statistical and texture-feature extraction.

## How I can contribute
I can support projects that require a bridge between **materials processing, analytical characterization, device engineering, and biomedical application**.
My strongest value is connecting measurements from different instruments with engineering questions such as film quality, sidewall coverage, texture, morphology, stress, and device suitability.

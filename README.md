# EU Umbrella

Personal job-discovery dashboard for identifying and ranking high-fit opportunities in Germany and Europe.

1. PhD / doctoral researcher positions:
   - Acoustic and ultrasonic MEMS
   - Piezoelectric materials and Aluminum Nitride (AlN)
   - Thin films, ALD, sputtering, and MEMS fabrication
   - FTIR, XRD, SEM, wafer bow, and surface/materials characterization
   - Biomedical microsystems, sensors, biomaterials, smart implants
   
2. Industry R&D positions:
   - MEMS / microsystems / sensor-development engineer
   - Thin-film or materials R&D engineer
   - Surface-characterization or laboratory engineer
   - Medical-device R&D, verification, validation, and quality engineer
   - Application engineer for FTIR, XRD, SEM, or materials-analysis instrumentation

3. Secondary directions:
   - Clinical engineering and medical-equipment engineering
   - Biomedical imaging, MATLAB/Python, and applied machine learning

## Geographic priorities

- Germany: highest priority
- Germany regions: Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, North Rhine-Westphalia, Berlin/Brandenburg, Saxony, and Hamburg
- Europe: Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, France, and Ireland

## Rule for applying

Only move a vacancy to the private application tracker when it has:
- A score of 70/100 or higher, or
- A strategic reason to apply despite a lower score.

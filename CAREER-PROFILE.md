# Optimized Career Profile

## Professional headline

**Biomedical Microsystems & Materials Engineer | MEMS, AlN Thin Films, Materials Characterization, Medical-Device QA and Validation**

## Profile summary

M.Sc. Biomedical Engineer specializing in materials-driven R&D at the intersection of microsystems and medical technology. Hands-on research experience characterizing AlN thin films on three-dimensional Si/SiO/PolySi MEMS structures using FTIR, XRD, SEM and wafer-bow measurements, including comparison of ALD and RF-magnetron-sputtered films and MATLAB-based optical simulation. Brings three years of clinical-equipment engineering experience covering installation, calibration, validation, preventive maintenance, quality documentation and failure analysis of diagnostic and critical-care systems. Positioned for early-career R&D, materials/process characterization, test/validation, quality, biomedical, medical-device, field-service and doctoral-research roles.

## Three target routes

### Semiconductor and materials

Use this route for MEMS, thin-film, process-development, characterization, metrology, test and semiconductor-adjacent positions. Lead with AlN, ALD versus sputtering, FTIR, XRD, SEM, wafer bow, residual stress, crystal orientation, morphology and MATLAB analysis.

### Biomedical and medtech

Use this route for medical-device, clinical-engineering, equipment, verification, validation, quality and field-service positions. Lead with three years of medical-equipment responsibility, calibration, performance validation, preventive maintenance, QA documentation, safety awareness and diagnostic-system exposure.

### Research and PhD

Use this route for experimental R&D, doctoral and research-engineer positions. Lead with the grade-1.0 thesis, independent measurement-workflow development, 90-degree sidewall FTIR setup, cross-method correlation, medical-imaging projects and evidence-based technical writing.

## Honest positioning

Do not claim IC design, semiconductor-fab ownership, production-software engineering, formal ISO 13485 auditor status or independent regulatory-submission leadership. For positions asking for these, frame your background as experimentally rigorous and transferable while identifying the specific gap you are prepared to close.

## Application priorities

Prioritize roles with technical fit of at least 65 and attainability of at least 55. Apply selectively below those thresholds only where the employer welcomes related degrees, equivalent research experience, graduate candidates or transferable laboratory/quality experience.

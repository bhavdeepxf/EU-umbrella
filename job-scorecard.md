# Job Fit Scorecard

Score each vacancy before applying.

## Total score: 100 points

| Criterion | Maximum points | How to score |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct technical match | 30 | AlN, MEMS, piezoelectric films, FTIR, XRD, SEM, wafer bow, ALD, sputtering, sensors |
| Role direction match | 20 | PhD, R&D, materials characterization, medical-device verification/validation, laboratory/application engineering |
| Evidence from my CV | 15 | Clear achievement or instrument/project evidence that I can discuss in an interview |
| Entry-level suitability | 10 | Suitable for MSc graduate, junior engineer, doctoral candidate, or 0–3 years experience |
| Location preference | 10 | Germany first; then selected European countries |
| Language fit | 5 | English accepted or German requirement matches my present level |
| Research/technology interest | 5 | Acoustic MEMS, biomaterials, smart implants, medical devices, imaging, sensors |
| Application practicality | 5 | Clear deadline, feasible documents, no mandatory experience gap |

## Score interpretation

| Score | Decision |
|---:|---|
| 85–100 | Apply immediately; high-priority role |
| 70–84 | Strong fit; tailor CV and cover letter |
| 55–69 | Apply selectively; identify gaps before applying |
| Below 55 | Save only if strategically useful or if requirements are flexible |

## Automatic priority boosts

Add these after calculating the total score:

- +5: Vacancy explicitly mentions AlN, piezoelectric films, or acoustic/ultrasonic MEMS.
- +5: Vacancy requires FTIR, XRD, SEM, thin-film characterization, or wafer-level analysis.
- +5: Vacancy is at a university, institute, or company in Baden-Württemberg.
- +3: Application is in English or explicitly welcomes international applicants.

Maximum final score: 110.

## Red flags

Reduce the score or do not apply when:

- The role requires a completed PhD.
- The role requires German C1/C2 for an essential customer-facing or clinical duty.
- The role requires more than five years of directly relevant industry experience.
- The vacancy is primarily software development with no biomedical, imaging, data, materials, or instrumentation link.
- The deadline is passed or the original vacancy page is unavailable.
